Model Transparency & Assumptions
  • Nutrients have a broad productive zone around 700–900 ppm, with declining performance outside it.
  • pH is modeled as most productive around 5.5–6.5.
  • Light response is strongest around 14–18 hr/day and declines with excessive photoperiod.
  • LED and incandescent use simplified classroom-scale power assumptions.
  • 1 simulated serving = 100 g harvest biomass; this is an educational conversion.
  • Operating cost includes modeled lighting electricity and nutrient use only.
